# Ship once

## Title

Reject a second shipment

## Goal

An order that has shipped cannot ship again.

## Context

SPEC-20261009-202 adopts the invariant from DOMAIN-MODEL-20261009-202 that an order ships once.

## Scope

Reject a second shipment of the same order. Cited inputs: SPEC-20261009-202 and DOMAIN-MODEL-20261009-202.

## Out of Scope

A Shipping aggregate. DOMAIN-MODEL-20261009-202 recommends one, and SPEC-20261009-202 does not adopt that recommendation, so it is not a ticket requirement.

## Dependencies

None.

## Implementation Notes

Use the adopted invariant only. Cited inputs: SPEC-20261009-202 and DOMAIN-MODEL-20261009-202.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A second shipment of the same order is rejected. Cited inputs: SPEC-20261009-202 and DOMAIN-MODEL-20261009-202.

## Validation

Attempt a second shipment and observe the rejection. Cited inputs: SPEC-20261009-202 and DOMAIN-MODEL-20261009-202.

## Definition of Done

- The rejection is observable and no aggregate was introduced. Cited inputs: SPEC-20261009-202 and DOMAIN-MODEL-20261009-202.
